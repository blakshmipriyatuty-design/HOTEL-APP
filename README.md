# StayNest – Hotel Booking App
## Project Overview
StayNest is a user-friendly hotel booking app that helps users find and book hotels easily. Users can search for destinations, explore hotel details, select rooms, choose AC or Non-AC options, specify their stay duration, and select food requirements. The app provides a clear booking summary and payment flow.

## Objectives
- Make hotel searching simple and convenient.
- Display hotel details, ratings, locations, and prices.
- Allow users to customize their room preferences.
- Provide flexible booking options for hours or days.
- Let users choose whether food is required.
- Simplify the booking summary and payment process.
- Create a clean, consistent, and user-friendly interface.

## Design Highlights
- **Colour Palette:** Navy blue, gold, white, cream, and light blue.
- **Typography:** Poppins for clear and consistent text.
- **Visual Style:** Clean, modern, and minimal design.
- **Components:** Hotel cards, search bars, buttons, icons, and selection controls.
- **Navigation:** Simple and intuitive screen flow.
- **Brand Identity:** StayNest logo with the tagline “Find • Book • Stay.”

## Features
- Destination search and popular destinations.
- Hotel listings with images, ratings, locations, and approximate prices.
- Room quantity selection.
- Single cot and double cot options.
- AC and Non-AC room selection.
- Stay duration selection by hours or days.
- Check-in and check-out date selection.
- Food requirement selection.
- Booking summary with room charges, food charges, and total amount.
- Payment options and booking confirmation.
- Settings, notifications, profile, and help support.
